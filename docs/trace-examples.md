# Four source-to-query traces

These examples were checked against the [English consolidated Act dated 27 July 2026](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A02024R1689-20260727), using the exact PDF hash in the README. The saved SPARQL files return the reviewed statements; they do not determine whether a real-world system satisfies a legal test.

| Question | Example and source check | Query result and modelling decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CQ1: prohibited practices | Article 5(1)(h) addresses use of real-time remote biometric identification in publicly accessible spaces for law enforcement. It states strictly necessary objectives and further conditions in Article 5(2)–(5). | `cq1.rq` returns the Article 5(1)(h) prohibition with its scope and exception text. The practice class is an `Article5ListedPractice`, not an unconditional subclass of `ProhibitedPractice`. |
| CQ2: high-risk categories | Article 6(2) treats Annex III-listed systems as high-risk, but Article 6(3) derogates when there is no significant risk and one listed circumstance applies; profiling of natural persons remains high-risk. Article 6(4) requires a provider invoking non-high-risk status to document its assessment and register. | `cq2.rq` returns all eight Annex III areas and the qualification. `AnnexIIIListedAISystem` is an `AISystem`, but ELK must **not** entail that it is a `HighRiskAISystem`. |
| CQ3: actor duties | Article 16(e) tells providers to keep automatically generated logs when under their control. Article 26(6) tells deployers to keep controlled logs for an appropriate period of at least six months, unless applicable law provides otherwise. | `cq3.rq` returns separate provider and deployer records, each linked to `HighRiskAISystem` and its own provision. It does not merge their retention terms. |
| CQ4: conditions and exceptions | Article 26(6) qualifies the deployer duty by control, intended purpose, minimum six-month period and applicable-law exception. Article 6(3) carries the separate high-risk derogation. | `cq4.rq` returns these statement annotations with their source references. The exceptions remain text, not executable applicability rules. |

The machine-readable [validation report](../reports/validation.json) records the profile, ELK entailment checks, consistency, cited-Article evidence matching, and query results. The Act's full provisions and their interactions still require expert legal review; these are targeted traces of the prototype.
